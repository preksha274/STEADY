## 1. What is this study about?
You are invited to take part in a research study testing a smartphone app called **STEADY**. The app is designed to help record movement patterns (like hand tapping, walking pace, and speech clarity) in people with Parkinson's Disease. 

We want to learn how easy and comfortable the app is to use in daily life, and how accurately its automated finger-tap scores match a doctor's live scoring.

---

## 2. Is taking part voluntary?
**Yes. Taking part is 100% voluntary.**
* You can choose whether or not to join.
* You can stop taking part at any time during the session.
* Choosing not to join or leaving the study will **not** affect your medical care or doctor visits in any way.

---

## 3. What will I be asked to do?
If you agree to take part, you will complete one 45-minute visit at our clinic with a doctor present:
1. **Hand Tapping**: Tap your index finger and thumb together for 10 seconds while a phone camera or sensor measures your speed.
2. **Arm Hold & Short Walk**: Hold your arms out for 10 seconds and walk down a short, clear hallway.
3. **Voice Sound**: Say "ahhh" steadily into the microphone for 3 seconds.
4. **Questions**: Answer a few short questions about how easy the app was to use.

---

## 4. What information is recorded?
The app records the following sensor signals during your session:
* **Motion data**: Hand and leg movement speed from wearable sensors or phone accelerometers.
* **Voice acoustics**: Pitch stability and volume control during your 3-second sound check.
* **Camera finger position**: Camera tracking of finger tap position during the 10-second tap test.
* **Location data**: **GPS location is OFF by default** and is only shared if you explicitly choose to turn it on.

---

## 5. Important Safety & Medical Notice
* ⚠️ **No Medical Decisions Made by App**: The STEADY app is an experimental research tool. It does **NOT** give medical diagnoses, change your prescriptions, or replace your doctor's advice.
* ⚠️ **You Can Delete Your Data**: If you decide to withdraw, you can ask us to permanently delete all your recorded sensor files at any time.

---

## 6. How is my privacy protected?
* Your real name and contact details are **never** stored with your movement data.
* You will be assigned a random code number (such as `ST-PLT-004`).
* All information is saved securely on encrypted storage. Only authorized researchers have access.
